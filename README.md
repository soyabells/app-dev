## My Favorite Movie Series: Twilight

**Genre:** Fantasy romance
**Number of films:** 5 (2008 to 2012)

### Why I like it
*Twilight* is my favorite because it mixes romance, mystery and fantasy in a small, rainy town, and the characters and soundtrack stay with me long after each film ends.

### Main characters
- **Bella Swan** – a quiet teenager who moves to Forks, Washington
- **Edward Cullen** – a vampire from the Cullen family who falls in love with Bella
- **Jacob Black** – Bella's close friend who belongs to the Quileute tribe

### The films in order
1. Twilight
2. New Moon
3. Eclipse
4. Breaking Dawn – Part 1
5. Breaking Dawn – Part 2

> A story about love, loyalty and the choices we make for the people we care about.
